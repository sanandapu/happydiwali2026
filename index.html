The Light Behind the Festival

  1. Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is thousands of years old and is celebrated across India and by communities all over the world.
  2. In the Ramayana, Lord Rama returned to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile and his victory over Ravana, and the city lit rows of clay lamps to welcome him home.
  3. Many also remember Krishna's victory over the demon Narakasura, and the festival night honours Goddess Lakshmi, giver of prosperity.
  4. Jains remember Lord Mahavira's nirvana, and Sikhs celebrate Bandi Chhor Divas, the release of Guru Hargobind Ji.
  5. The celebration lasts five days: Dhanteras, Naraka Chaturdashi, Lakshmi Puja, Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj.
  6. Everywhere the message is the same: light over darkness, good over evil, knowledge over ignorance, shown in diyas, rangoli, sweets and shared joy.
